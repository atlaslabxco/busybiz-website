BEFORE WE GET PLAYING

A few good
questions.

Can the game match my brand?

Yes. The colours, copy, creative and reward mechanics can be shaped around your business. The demos show possible starting points.

Do I need creators for every campaign?

No. A campaign can run through your own channels, with creators, or a combination. We’ll scope the approach around your audience and goals.

What does a campaign cost?

Campaigns are custom-quoted. We’ll discuss the game, production, creator involvement, prizes and reporting before preparing a proposal.

How long does it take to launch?

The timeline depends on the game and campaign scope. We’ll agree the build, review and launch milestones with you before work starts.

What about prizes and promotion requirements?

Prize arrangements, promotion terms, relevant permit requirements, creator disclosures and data handling need to be agreed as part of the campaign scope before launch.
